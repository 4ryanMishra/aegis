## Selection rule
Do not choose a method because it sounds sophisticated. Select methods that are:
1. reproducible;
2. source-transparent;
3. suitable for a one-hour horizon;
4. empirically testable;
5. robust to abnormal observations;
6. explainable to a finance/security judge.
